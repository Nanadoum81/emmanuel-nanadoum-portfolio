What brings people in, and what happens next

Every visit starts with a conversation. If chiropractic care isn't the right answer for you, the doctors will say so plainly.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Low back pain, strain and spasmIncluding pain that has lingered for months
  • Neck pain and stiffnessFrom desks, driving and sleeping wrong
  • Headaches that start in the neckOften after an old injury
  • Disc problemsEvaluated before any adjustment
  • Sports and active-life strainsTo keep you training, working, moving
  • After a car accidentRecords can be shared with your physician

How the doctors treat

  • Chiropractic adjustmentsHands-on spinal manipulation, explained before it happens
  • Stretch routines for homeSo progress continues between visits
  • Electrical muscle stimulation (TENS)Used where it helps muscles settle
  • Sports chiropracticCare that respects what you ask of your body

Chiropractic care is not a cure for disease, and results differ from person to person.

Your first visit

  1. Before

    Call or send a request. The office confirms a time with you by phone and can check your insurance before you come in.

  2. At the visit

    You talk with the doctor about what's bothering you and your history, then get an honest assessment of whether chiropractic care fits.

  3. After

    If care makes sense, the plan is explained at your pace. Ongoing visits are always your choice.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your insurance card
  • A list of current medications
  • Any X-rays, MRI reports or notes from other doctors
  • Comfortable clothes you can move in
