The Practice

Cactus Chiropractic

Cactus Chiropractic Clinic LLC is a Peoria-based chiropractic practice led by Dr. Catherine Devore-Meehan, D.C.. The focus is simple: listen first, then build an individualized approach around your concerns.

Visits are deliberately unhurried, so there's time to ask questions and discuss your situation with the chiropractor.
