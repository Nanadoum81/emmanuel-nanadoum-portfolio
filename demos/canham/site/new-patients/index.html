New Patients

Starting care is simple.

Welcome. Whether this is your first chiropractic visit or you're looking for a new practice, here's what to expect when you begin care with Dr. Canham.

01

Request Your Visit

Submit a quick appointment request online, or call the practice directly. We'll follow up to confirm a time.

02

Your First Appointment

An unhurried visit to discuss your history, what brings you in, and what you're hoping to feel.

03

A Plan for You

Dr. Canham outlines what ongoing care could look like — shaped around your goals, not a template.

04

Ongoing Care

A steady rhythm of supportive visits that adjusts as your needs and how you feel change over time.

Good to Know

Before your visit.

  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing you can move in.
  • Arrive a few minutes early so your first visit isn't rushed.
  • Bring any questions — Dr. Canham takes time to answer them.
  • If you have relevant past records, you're welcome to bring them along.
  • No referral required to reach out — call or request online anytime.

Insurance & Fees

Insurance participation, accepted plans, and visit fees should be confirmed directly with the practice before your appointment.

Scheduling

Visits are by appointment. Request a day and time online and we'll follow up to confirm what works.

Prefer to talk?

The team is happy to walk you through what to expect and confirm current details before you come in.
