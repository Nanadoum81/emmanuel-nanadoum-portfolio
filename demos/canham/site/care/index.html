Care & Services

Hands-on chiropractic, tailored to you.

Dr. Canham offers personal chiropractic care focused on attentive assessment and intentional adjustment. Below is a general overview; your actual visit is shaped around your needs.

Chiropractic Adjustment

Hands-on spinal adjustments tailored to your body, comfort, and goals. The foundation of the care Dr. Canham provides.

Adjustments are performed thoughtfully, with attention to how your body responds — not a routine applied the same way to everyone.

Supportive Wellness Care

Ongoing visits designed to help you maintain how you feel between episodes of tension, rather than only reacting to them.

For many patients, supportive care becomes a steady rhythm — a way to stay ahead of discomfort rather than chase it.

New Patient Consultation

An unhurried first visit to understand your history, what brings you in, and what you're hoping to achieve.

This is where listening happens. Your goals shape the conversation, and the care that follows.
