About

Care delivered one patient at a time.

Dr. Rosalind L. Canham, DC

Dr. Rosalind L. Canham is a chiropractor serving patients from her practice on North 7th Street in Phoenix, Arizona.

The practice is built on a simple idea: that chiropractic care should feel personal. Each visit is an opportunity to listen carefully, assess thoughtfully, and adjust with intention — never to rush.

For some patients that means focused relief from everyday tension. For others it means a steady rhythm of supportive wellness visits. In every case, the care is shaped around the person in front of Dr. Canham, not a template.

A note on accuracy: Detailed credentials, education, and professional history should be confirmed directly with the practice. This page intentionally avoids stating specifics that have not been verified.
