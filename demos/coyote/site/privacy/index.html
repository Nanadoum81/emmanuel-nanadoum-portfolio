Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Coyote Chiropractic and Wellness Center, P.C. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit an appointment request or contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, your preferred contact method, and any message you provide. We do not request sensitive medical information through this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to contact you about your appointment request, respond to your inquiries, and provide or coordinate care. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying information to improve our website.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is fully secure. Please do not send confidential medical details through this website.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, though that may limit our ability to respond to your request. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, please contact us directly.
