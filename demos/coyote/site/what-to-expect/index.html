Your First Visit

Knowing what to expect makes it easier.

From your first call to ongoing care, here is how things typically unfold at Coyote Chiropractic.

1. Schedule your visit

Call the office or request an appointment online. We will help you find a time that works and answer any initial questions you have.

2. Arrive & check in

Plan to arrive a few minutes early for your first appointment so we can get your information recorded comfortably — no rush.

3. History & consultation

Dr. Merchant will sit down with you to understand your concerns, history, and what brought you in. This conversation guides everything that follows.

4. Chiropractic evaluation

A hands-on evaluation assesses how your body is moving. Dr. Merchant explains what he is checking and why as he goes.

5. Findings & recommendations

You will hear a clear explanation of the findings and a discussion of what care could look like — with your questions welcomed throughout.

6. Your care, at your pace

If you choose to move forward, care progresses at a pace that makes sense for you, with honest check-ins about how things are going.

New Patients

New here? You are in good hands.

Starting with a new chiropractor can feel uncertain. We keep the first visit straightforward and unhurried, with plenty of time for your questions. There is no obligation to begin care — the goal of the first visit is simply to understand your situation and explore what is possible.
