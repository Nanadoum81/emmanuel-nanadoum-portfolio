Chiropractic Care

Spinal adjustments for mobility, comfort & function

Hands-on chiropractic adjustments focused on the relationship between your spine and nervous system, supporting how your body moves and feels day to day.

The problem it addresses

Stiffness, tension, and restricted movement can build quietly from everyday posture, activity, and wear. Many people live with reduced mobility or comfort long before they consider professional care.

When you might need it

If you are noticing ongoing tightness, reduced range of motion, or a sense that your body is not moving the way it should, a chiropractic evaluation is a reasonable next step.

Why professional care matters

Adjustments are skilled, specific techniques. Dr. Merchant takes time to understand your history and explain what he is doing and why, so care feels informed rather than routine.
