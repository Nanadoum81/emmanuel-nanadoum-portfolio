Systems

Eleven connected components

Website Lead Capture

Every appointment request flows directly into the CRM as a tagged lead with a pipeline opportunity.

AI / Voice Receptionist

A future human-sounding receptionist to handle calls, answer FAQs, capture leads, and transfer to staff. Not yet activated.

Missed Call Recovery

When a call is missed, an automatic text-back goes out within about a minute to re-open the conversation.

Appointment Booking

Requests route to the Appointment Requested stage and trigger immediate confirmation by text and email.

Lead Pipeline

A 12-stage pipeline tracks every contact from New Lead through Completed, Reactivation, and Lost.

Automated Follow-Up

A measured sequence follows up with unbooked leads — then stops the moment they reply, book, or opt out.

Appointment Reminders

Automated reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit, including time, address, and phone.

No-Show Recovery

If an appointment is marked No Show or Cancelled, a polite reschedule offer is sent automatically.

Patient Reactivation

A separate, manually-activated workflow reaches out to inactive patients with a simple invitation to return.

Review Requests

After a completed visit, a polite, ungated review request is sent. No incentives, no gating.

Reporting

Pipeline movement, lead source, and conversion visibility across the full patient journey.

Workflows

Automation logic, by example

Website Lead → Confirmation

  1. 01Form submitted on the website
  2. 02Contact created/updated, tagged ph-website-lead
  3. 03Opportunity added to Palmer & Herman Patient Pipeline → Appointment Requested
  4. 04Internal notification to the office
  5. 05Immediate confirmation text sent to the patient
  6. 06Confirmation email sent to the patient
  7. 07Follow-up task created if no appointment is booked

Missed Call Text-Back

  1. 01Incoming call is missed
  2. 02Wait ~1 minute
  3. 03Send: "Hi, this is Palmer & Herman Chiropractic. Sorry we missed your call. How can we help you today?"
  4. 04If they respond → move to Conversation Started + notify office
  5. 05If no response → follow up again later (reasonable, not repeated)

New Lead Follow-Up Sequence

  1. 01Immediate: thanks for reaching out, we received your request
  2. 02Several hours later: checking in — would you like help finding a time?
  3. 03Next day: happy to help when you're ready, reply or call (203) 729-4047
  4. 04Stops when: appointment booked, lead replies, lead opts out, or marked lost

Appointment Reminders

  1. 0124 hours before: appointment time, office address, phone number
  2. 022 hours before: appointment time, office address, phone number

No-Show / Cancellation Recovery

  1. 01Appointment status becomes No Show or Cancelled
  2. 02Send: "Hi {first_name}, we noticed your appointment didn't work out today. If you'd like to reschedule, reply here and we'll help you find another time."

Patient Reactivation (manual)

  1. 01Manually activated on selected inactive patients
  2. 02Send: "Hi {first_name}, this is Palmer & Herman Chiropractic. It's been a while since we've seen you. If you'd like to schedule a visit, reply here and we'll help you find a convenient time."

Review Request

  1. 01Trigger: completed appointment
  2. 02Wait a reasonable period after the visit
  3. 03Send a polite review request — no incentives, no gating

Future Capability

AI voice receptionist — ready to activate

The system is prepared for a future human-sounding voice receptionist. It is not yet activated. When enabled, it would handle incoming calls, office information, appointment requests, basic FAQs, directions, hours, appointment changes, lead capture, and human transfer.

Planned greeting

"Thanks for calling Palmer & Herman Chiropractic. How can I help you today?"

The receptionist would never claim to be an AI, never make medical diagnoses, never provide medical advice, and would escalate medical questions to staff.
