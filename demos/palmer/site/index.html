The Practice

Palmer & Herman Chiropractic

Palmer & Herman Chiropractic is a Naugatuck-based practice led by Dr. Terry A. Palmer, DC and Dr. Glenn S. Herman, DC. The focus is simple: listen first, then build an individualized approach around your concerns.

Visits are deliberately unhurried, so there's time to ask questions and discuss your situation directly with the chiropractors.
