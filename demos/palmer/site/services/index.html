Care Areas

Chiropractic care, explained plainly

These are the common reasons patients come to Palmer & Herman. Specific care is always discussed during your visit and tailored to you. Chiropractic care does not cure disease, and individual results vary.

01

Chiropractic Evaluation

An initial assessment is the starting point for most patients. During an evaluation, the doctor reviews your concerns, discusses your history, and considers whether chiropractic care may be appropriate for you. This is a conversation as much as an assessment — you're encouraged to ask questions and understand the reasoning behind any recommendation.

  • Discussion of your concerns and goals
  • Review of relevant history
  • An honest assessment of whether care is appropriate
02

Movement & Mobility Support

How your body moves day to day matters. Attention to movement and mobility looks at practical, sustainable patterns — the way you sit, stand, work, and recover. The goal is care that fits your life rather than disrupting it.

  • Focus on everyday movement patterns
  • Practical, sustainable approach
  • Care that fits your routine
03

Back & Neck Concerns

Back and neck concerns are among the most common reasons patients seek chiropractic care. These are evaluated individually and discussed openly. No two people present the same way, and care is approached accordingly.

  • Individual evaluation of your concern
  • Open, plain-language discussion
  • No one-size-fits-all recommendations
04

Sports & Active Lifestyle Support

For patients with active routines, care considers the demands you place on your body — from work and training to recreation. The aim is to support continued activity rather than sideline it.

  • Care that considers your activity level
  • Support for continued movement
  • Attention to repetitive demands
05

Ongoing Chiropractic Care

Some patients choose to continue care over time as part of their overall wellness routine. Ongoing care is always your choice, discussed openly, and never pressured. The frequency and approach are tailored to you.

  • Optional, patient-directed continuity
  • Tailored frequency and approach
  • No pressure or obligation
06

New Patient Consultation

If you're exploring chiropractic for the first time, a consultation is a low-pressure way to learn about the practice, meet the team, and ask questions before deciding on next steps.

  • A starting point for first-time patients
  • Space to ask questions
  • No obligation to begin care
