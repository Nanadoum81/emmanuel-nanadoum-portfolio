Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered plainly.

Here are the things patients most often want to know before their first visit. Don't see your question? Just call the office.

How do I request an appointment?

You can request an appointment through the contact form on this site, or call the office directly at (480) 966-1635. We'll follow up to confirm a time that works for you.

Where is the office located?

Nelson Chiropractic is located at 2409 S Rural Rd #D, Tempe, AZ 85282, on Rural Road in Tempe.

What should I expect at a first visit?

Your first visit starts with a conversation about what brought you in and how you're feeling. From there, the doctor determines whether chiropractic care is appropriate for you and discusses a plan. There's no pressure — clear, straightforward communication comes first.

Do I need a referral to come in?

A referral is generally not required to request an appointment. If your situation involves an accident or insurance claim, we can discuss any documentation that may be helpful when you call.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy movement. Bring a photo ID and any relevant accident or referral paperwork if your visit is accident-related.

Is care appropriate after a car accident?

Chiropractic care is commonly sought after auto accidents. If you've been in an accident, call the office so we can understand your situation and discuss next steps before your visit.

How long are visits?

First visits typically take a bit longer to allow time for discussion and assessment. Follow-up visits are generally shorter and focused. We'll give you a realistic expectation when we confirm your appointment.

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk it through before you book.
