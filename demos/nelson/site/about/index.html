About the Practice

A neighborhood practice, built on attention.

Nelson Chiropractic is a local Tempe practice offering personalized, hands-on chiropractic care. The focus is simple: listen first, communicate clearly, and provide professional care in a setting that feels comfortable rather than clinical.

Care here is shaped around the person in front of the doctor — not a rigid protocol. That means time to talk through what you're experiencing, an honest assessment of whether chiropractic care fits your situation, and straightforward guidance on what comes next.

Donald L. Nelson, DC

Chiropractor · Tempe, Arizona

Dr. Donald L. Nelson provides chiropractic care at the practice on South Rural Road in Tempe. Patients are seen one-on-one, with each visit beginning from a conversation about how they're feeling and what brought them in.

The approach is direct and unhurried: assess whether chiropractic care is appropriate, explain what that care involves, and let the patient decide how to move forward — without pressure.
