Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Nelson Chiropractic ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how information you provide through this website is handled. This website is a marketing and appointment-request tool; it is not a patient portal and should not be used to transmit protected health information.

Information You Provide

When you submit the appointment-request form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, a reason for your visit, a preferred contact method, and an optional message. We use this information to contact you about your appointment request and to respond to your inquiry.

Health Information

Please do not include sensitive medical details, diagnoses, or protected health information in the website form. Any clinical information needed for your care will be collected privately and in accordance with applicable law during your visit.

How We Use Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your request, schedule or discuss appointments, and communicate with you about the practice. We do not sell your information.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request that we update or remove your contact information from our marketing records, subject to any records we are required to retain.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Nelson Chiropractic at 2409 S Rural Rd #D, Tempe, AZ 85282 or by phone at (480) 966-1635.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
