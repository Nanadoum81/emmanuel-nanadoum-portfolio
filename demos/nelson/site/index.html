Common Questions

What patients usually ask.

How do I request an appointment?

You can request an appointment through the contact form on this site, or call the office directly at (480) 966-1635. We'll follow up to confirm a time that works for you.

Where is the office located?

Nelson Chiropractic is located at 2409 S Rural Rd #D, Tempe, AZ 85282, on Rural Road in Tempe.

What should I expect at a first visit?

Your first visit starts with a conversation about what brought you in and how you're feeling. From there, the doctor determines whether chiropractic care is appropriate for you and discusses a plan. There's no pressure — clear, straightforward communication comes first.

Do I need a referral to come in?

A referral is generally not required to request an appointment. If your situation involves an accident or insurance claim, we can discuss any documentation that may be helpful when you call.
